Honey that turns solid is honey that’s real

Pure honey tends to crystallize. It’s natural and easy to reverse.

If it crystallizes

Keep the bottle closed and sit it in warm water. It returns to its smooth texture.

Storing it

  • Keep it at room temperature.
  • Do not store honey in the refrigerator.
  • Close the cap after every use.

Questions

Pure honey crystallizes naturally. Sit the closed bottle in warm water and it loosens up again.

Can I keep it in the fridge?

No. Keep it at room temperature.
